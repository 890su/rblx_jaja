# Roblox Monetization for Small Developers (as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-11. Data that predates 2025 is labeled with its date. Third-party revenue trackers (RoWatcher, profitable.app, RoLearn) use their own models, disagree with each other, and are only rough estimates. The only hard numbers come from Roblox itself (newsroom, SEC filings, Creator Hub docs).

## 1. Monetization tools in 2026 and changes in 2025–2026

### Takeaway
The main tools are still game passes (one-time purchases) and developer products (repeatable purchases), plus experience subscriptions, private servers, rewarded video and immersive ads, regional pricing and the price-optimization tool. Several things changed in 2025–2026:
- Engagement-Based (Premium) Payouts were replaced by the **Creator Rewards Program** (July 2025).
- **DevEx went up 8.5%** (Sept 2025).
- **Regional pricing** launched (April 2025).
- **Roblox Plus**, a $4.99/mo player subscription, launched (April 2026).
- DevEx is **+42% for spending by age-checked US 18+ players** (from June 2026).
- Ad rules were tightened, including no rewarded ads for under-13s (May 2026). Roblox will take a share of brand-deal revenue from January 2027.
- **Roblox Wallet** (daily cash payouts) was announced at RDC 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Creator Rewards Program** replaced the legacy Engagement-Based Payouts Program, effective July 24, 2025. The old program paid creators by hours that Premium subscribers spent in their experience. The new one pays Robux for hitting engagement and monetization metrics, such as "daily engagement rewards" and bringing in new and returning users. Roblox expects total creator earnings under Creator Rewards to exceed EBP (a forward-looking statement, not a reported result). — [Roblox 10-Q Q2 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000262/rblx-20250630.htm); [Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- **Experience subscriptions:** creators earn Robux from selling virtual content or access, including through subscriptions. Creators can also give Premium subscribers discounted items or exclusive features. — [Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- **DevEx +8.5%** for all Robux earned from September 5, 2025, announced at RDC 2025. — [Roblox press release via Placera, 2025-09-05](https://www.placera.se/pressmeddelanden/roblox-a-roblox-unveils-ai-monetization-and-performance-innovations-for-creators-20250905); [10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- **DevEx +42% for qualifying 18+ spend** (announced April 30, 2026, effective June 8, 2026):
  - Covers spending by age-checked US players aged 18+ in eligible games that use R15 avatars.
  - Qualifying purchases: game passes, Robux subscriptions, select in-game items and private servers.
  - Roblox says the US 18–34 cohort "monetizes over 50% higher" than under-18 users and grew more than 50% YoY. These are estimates extrapolated from age-checked data.
  — [Roblox Newsroom, 2026-04](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42)
- **Regional pricing** for in-experience items launched in April 2025:
  - The creator sets a base price, and Roblox adjusts it for each local economy. Creators choose which items are priced regionally.
  - Roblox's own early test reported higher spending in Mexico (+17%), Brazil (+26%) and the Philippines (+52%). Roblox said early testing showed "zero cases" of arbitrage abuse.
  — [Roblox Newsroom 2025-04](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/04/roblox-launches-regional-pricing-for-in-experience-items); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/04/22/roblox-regional-pricing-in-experience-items/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-introduces-regional-pricing/)
- **Price Optimization tool:** runs A/B price tests and recommends prices. Roblox says it raised median creator earnings by about 4% after its 2024 rollout. It can be combined with regional pricing. — [Tubefilter, 2025-04](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/04/22/roblox-regional-pricing-in-experience-items/)
- **Rewarded Video ads** (players opt in to 30-second ads for in-game rewards, Google partnership, April 2025):
  - In August 2025, self-serve access required: publisher eligibility, an average of at least 100K DAU over 28 days, minimal or mild content maturity, and good standing. Roblox planned to widen access later; I could not confirm that it did.
  - Shown only to users aged 13+.
  - Creator revenue share is driven by advertiser demand.
  — [DevForum: More creators can now use Rewarded Video Ads](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/more-creators-can-now-use-rewarded-video-ads/3838678); [Roblox Newsroom 2025-04](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/04/roblox-scales-video-ads-partners-with-google)
- **Immersive ads requirements:**
  - The creator must be 13+, ID-verified and have 2FA enabled.
  - The game must be public.
  - Roblox must approve the Maturity and Compliance Questionnaire.
  - Payouts arrive automatically on the 25th of the following month.
  — [Creator Hub: Immersive ads](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/immersive-ads). Some secondary 2025 coverage says owners must be 18+, which conflicts with the official docs.
- **Roblox Plus** (announced April 2026, launched globally April 30, 2026):
  - Player subscription at $4.99/mo that gives discounts on in-game and avatar purchases.
  - Roblox covers the discount, so creators earn the same per item.
  - Roblox Plus incentives have generated more than 300M Robux in creator earnings since launch (as of RDC, Sept 2026).
  — [Nasdaq/Roblox PR](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/roblox-announces-roblox-plus-new-subscription-plan-offering-exclusive-platform); [PocketGamer.biz RDC 2026](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-unveils-new-play-creation-and-monetisation-tools-at-rdc-2026/)
- **RDC 2026 (Sept 2026):**
  - Roblox Wallet: eligible US independent creators aged 18+ receive earnings in real currency every business day. GamesBeat says rollout starts December 2026, with expansion to groups, LLCs, minors and other countries planned over the following year.
  - Roblox Card is planned for 2027.
  - Players can buy avatar items seen in "Moments" from October 2026.
  - "Build" (prompt-to-game) is getting analytics and monetization tools, with mobile ads "coming".
  — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-unveils-new-play-creation-and-monetisation-tools-at-rdc-2026/); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-unveils-creator-wallets-and-physical-cards-rdc-2026/); [MassivelyOP](https://massivelyop.com/2026/09/15/roblox-unveils-plans-for-standalone-app-creation-a-direct-payment-wallet-and-more-gen-ai-tools/)

### Inferences
- For a small dev, the effective toolkit is game passes, developer products, regional pricing plus price optimization (both free to turn on), Creator Rewards (passive engagement income) and possibly subscriptions.
- Rewarded video ads are effectively out of reach below about 100K DAU (as of Aug 2025), and from May 2026 they cannot be shown to under-13s.
- The +42% 18+ rate rewards games that appeal to adults. Simple kid-skewed "brainrot"-type games mostly will not benefit.

### Gaps
- No public numbers on how much Creator Rewards pays per DAU or per engaged user.
- No eCPM or per-view creator payout figures for rewarded video.
- I could not confirm whether rewarded video access widened below 100K DAU after August 2025.

## 2. DevEx rate and how much of a player's dollar the developer keeps

### Takeaway
DevEx rates are:
- **Standard:** $0.0038/Robux ($380 per 100K Robux) since September 5, 2025.
- **Qualifying US 18+ spend:** $0.0054/Robux ($540 per 100K).
- **Robux earned before September 5, 2025:** $0.0035.

Roblox takes 30% of Robux spent on game passes and developer products. After app-store fees and the gap between the Robux purchase price and the DevEx rate, a developer receives roughly **20–30% of the dollars a player spends**.

### Cited Findings
- **Rates:**
  - Standard: 0.0038 USD per Earned Robux ($380/100K).
  - US 18+ rate: 0.0054 ($540/100K). Applies to developer products, passes, subscriptions and private servers bought by US players verified 18+ (facial age estimation or government ID) in eligible games.
  - Legacy rate: 0.0035 ($350/100K) for Robux earned before Sept 5, 2025. Old-rate balances are cashed out first.
  — [Creator Hub: DevEx](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange)
- **DevEx eligibility:**
  - Minimum cashout is 30,000 Earned Robux (about $114).
  - Age 13+, verified email, a DevEx portal account and a W-9 or W-8 on file.
  - One cashout per calendar month.
  — [Creator Hub: DevEx](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange)
- **Worked example:** a 400-Robux pass (about $5 retail) gives the developer 280 Robux after the 30% marketplace fee. 280 × $0.0035 = $0.98, so about 80% of the player's money goes to Roblox and app stores combined. This uses the old rate; at $0.0038 it is about $1.06. — [RoWatcher DevEx Math 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/devex-math-in-2026-what-you-actually-take-home-per-1-000-players). The article still uses $0.0035 even though the official rate is now $0.0038.
- **Split estimate:** about 25–30 cents of each player dollar reaches the developer, after roughly 23% app-store fees and roughly 48% Roblox share. — [RoWatcher Income Ladder](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-roblox-income-ladder-what-developers-really-earn)
- **Platform payouts:**
  - Creators earned more than $1.5B through DevEx in 2025. — [Roblox Newsroom 2026-04](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42)
  - About $1.7B in the 12 months before RDC 2026, and more than $5B since 2013. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-unveils-new-play-creation-and-monetisation-tools-at-rdc-2026/)
  - Over 1 year to June 30, 2025: more than $1B paid out, more than 29,000 creators in DevEx, median DevEx creator received $1,440, and the average Top-1,000 developer earned almost $1M. — [Roblox PR via Placera](https://www.placera.se/pressmeddelanden/roblox-a-roblox-unveils-ai-monetization-and-performance-innovations-for-creators-20250905)
  - 2024 (older data): $922.8M paid to developers and a median payout of $1,575. Over June 2024–July 2025, the top 10 developers averaged $38.5M/yr. — [RoWatcher Income Ladder](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-roblox-income-ladder-what-developers-really-earn) (secondary)

### Inferences
- **Rule of thumb:** 1 Robux spent in-game by a player is about $0.0027 to the developer at the standard rate (0.7 × 0.0038), or about $0.0038 for qualifying 18+ US spend.
- 1M Robux of gross in-game sales is about $2,660 to the developer.
- The median DevEx creator earns about $1.4K/yr, so the income distribution is extremely top-heavy.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm exact private-server fee splits or the subscription revenue share from official docs in this pass.

## 3. Benchmarks: payer conversion, ARPDAU, ARPPU

### Takeaway
Roblox does not publish per-genre benchmarks. Its Creator Dashboard shows peer benchmark scorecards to experiences with at least 100 DAU, but the values are not public.

Platform-wide (Q2 2026):
- Average bookings per DAU: **$12.66/quarter**, about $0.14 per DAU per day, gross.
- **27M monthly unique payers** against 123M DAU.
- **$19.25/month per payer.**

A developer's ARPDAU after fees and DevEx is a small fraction of these. Third-party estimates put it at **$0.003–$0.006** for weakly monetized games and **$0.015–$0.025** for well-monetized ones.

### Cited Findings
- **Q2 2026 platform figures:** revenue $1.5B (+36% YoY), bookings $1.6B (+8%), average DAU 123M (+10% YoY, down from the 152M peak in Q3 2025), ABPDAU $12.66 (−2% YoY), 27M average monthly unique payers (+15%) spending about $19.25 each.
- **Why bookings lagged:** Roblox said engagement moved from "high monetizing, 2025-vintage viral games" to new and evergreen games that earn less per hour, especially among younger users in the US and Canada. Changes to the Recommended For You algorithm, which favors retentive games, also hurt near-term monetization.
— [Roblox Q2 2026 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-revenue-rises-36-yy-but-young-north-americans-see-falling-per-hour-monetisation/); [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150208/roblox-daus-grew-10-percent-yoy)
- **Roblox's metric definitions:** conversion rate is the % of DAU who pay that day, and ARPDAU is revenue per DAU. Benchmark scorecards that compare an experience to similar ones are available at 100+ DAU. — [Creator Hub: Monetization analytics](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/analytics/monetization)
- The dashboard's 50th-percentile ARPDAU benchmark once showed 0. Roblox called it a data bug and fixed it. — [DevForum 2024](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetization-benchmarks-are-not-accurate/3006968)
- **Developer anecdote:** payer conversion and ARPDAU fell as the game grew. Commenters said new and broad-funnel players spend less. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/payer-conversion-rate-and-arpdau-decreasing-as-game-gains-more-popularity/3898921)
- **Developer-net ARPDAU estimates (after fees and DevEx):** $0.003–$0.006 conservative (for example, a mid-tier simulator or obby with a VIP pass) and $0.015–$0.025 well-monetized. Assumes DAU ≈ 4 × CCU. The author is a tracking-tool vendor and cites no data source. — [RoWatcher DevEx Math 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/devex-math-in-2026-what-you-actually-take-home-per-1-000-players)
- **Revenue per visit:** 0.03 Robux/visit for one 3,000-CCU game, said to be 3–5x below well-optimized competitors. — [RoWatcher Income Ladder](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-roblox-income-ladder-what-developers-really-earn)
- **Tracker model assumptions:** profitable.app assumes a 2% engagement (payer) rate, 150 Robux average spend per session, a 70% developer share and $0.0038/Robux. This is a model input, not measured data. — [profitable.app](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/grow-a-garden)
- **Age:** the US 18–34 cohort monetizes more than 50% higher than under-18 users. — [Roblox Newsroom 2026-04](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42). Another source puts 18+ spend per user at about +40%. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-roblox-income-ladder-what-developers-really-earn)

### Inferences
- Rough platform-level payer ratio: 27M monthly payers / 123M DAU is about 22%. That compares monthly payers to daily users, so daily payer conversion is much lower, plausibly low single digits. This is an inference, not a published figure.
- Platform gross bookings of about $0.14/DAU/day × about 0.27 developer share ≈ $0.04/DAU/day across all spending (avatar items included). That fits a top-game ceiling. Small games at $0.003–$0.025 are 2–10x below it.

### Gaps
- No published genre-level conversion, ARPDAU or ARPPU from Roblox, Naavik, Deconstructor of Fun or Gamefam/Bloxbiz turned up in this search window.
- Day-level payer conversion benchmarks (for example, "1–3% of DAU") are not published by Roblox.

## 4. Best-selling products in hit games and typical Robux prices

### Takeaway
Hits combine these monetization pieces:
- Cheap permanent multiplier passes: 2x Money, VIP, roughly 99–499 R$.
- Repeatable consumables: seed/egg packs, luck boosts, "grow all" or skip items, roughly 37–400 R$.
- Seasonal battle passes: about 749–849 R$.
- Permanent big-ticket items: 1,000–5,000 R$, such as a permanent fruit or admin commands.
- Limited-time packs and "server luck" boosts.

Reliable public data on *which* products sell best does not exist. Prices below come from game wikis and guides and change often.

### Cited Findings

  - A few prestige items at 1,000+ R$.
- Escalating-price infinite packs can backfire on player sentiment (the Grow a Garden Forever Pack).

## 5. Policy constraints: paid random items, ads to under-13s, pay-to-win

### Takeaway
Paid random items (anything random bought with Robux or with currency bought with Robux) must show every outcome with numerical odds that sum to exactly 100% **before purchase**. This includes indirect randomness (for example, seed → random plant) and luck boosts or pity systems.

Rewarded ads cannot target under-13s (from May 2026). Some brand categories are banned for under-13s, and all brand integrations must be registered with Roblox.

I found no explicit Roblox rule against pay-to-win. It is a community and reputation issue, not a policy violation.

### Cited Findings
- **Paid random items rules:**
  - Disclose all possible outcomes and actual numerical odds as percentages summing to 100%.
  - This applies to capsules, enhancement and combination items, and probability modifiers such as luck boosts and pity systems.
  - Indirect randomness is covered: if a Robux-bought seed is later planted for a random result, odds must be shown before planting.
  - A bare (i) icon with no descriptive label is not an acceptable disclosure trigger.
  — [Creator Hub: Paid random items](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items) (doc last updated in 2026)
- **Ad rules (DevForum announcement March 20, 2026, reported by Reuters):**
  - From May 4, 2026: rewarded ad formats are prohibited for users under 13. Food, cosmetics, pharmaceutical and financial-services brands are prohibited for under-13s.
  - All brand integrations must be registered and moderated before going live.
  - From January 2027, Roblox will take a revenue share from in-game brand deals.
  — [DZRH/Reuters, 2026-03-21](https://www.dzrh.com.ph/post/roblox-to-overhaul-ad-policies-introduce-revenue-sharing-in-2027)
- **Age checks:** facial age estimation was rolled out in November 2025, mainly to gate chat and communication. I found no source linking it to limits on paid random items. — [Bloomberg 2025-11-18](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2025-11-18/roblox-asks-users-to-verify-age-with-photo-based-tool-video)

### Inferences
- Simple trend games built on eggs, seeds or gacha must build an odds UI from day one. Luck-boost products also count as probability modifiers and need disclosure.
- Ad income is a poor fit for kid-heavy audiences after May 2026.

### Gaps
- I found no source listing countries where Roblox itself blocks paid random items (Roblox-specific regional loot-box restrictions, for example Belgium or the Netherlands).
- No explicit Roblox policy text on pay-to-win.

## 6. Mid-tier game revenue (1k–10k CCU) and rule-of-thumb revenue per CCU

### Takeaway
Estimates vary by more than 10x depending on design. Plausible developer-net ranges at 1K CCU:
- Weakly monetized (pass-only): about **$0.01–0.03 per CCU per day**, roughly $350–$1,750/mo.
- Well-monetized simulator or tycoon: about **$0.06–0.10+ per CCU per day**, roughly $2K–3K+/mo.

At 10K CCU, estimates range from about $50K/yr (social hangout) to more than $1M/yr (deep-progression RPG). All of these come from third-party trackers, not audited data.

### Cited Findings
- **RoWatcher DevEx Math 2026:**
  - DAU ≈ 4 × CCU.
  - At 1,000 CCU: $360–$720/mo (conservative) or $1,800–$3,000/mo (well-monetized), pre-tax.
  - About 800 CCU is needed to match US federal minimum wage (about $15K/yr).
  - Below 200–300 CCU, retention and visibility matter more than monetization.
  — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/devex-math-in-2026-what-you-actually-take-home-per-1-000-players)
- **RoWatcher Income Ladder (estimates):**
  - About 100 CCU: $190–$570/yr typical, $1,000–$1,500/yr with good passes.
  - 500–1,000 CCU with passes only: 1,000–1,250 R$/day.
  - About 1,000 CCU, well-designed simulator or tycoon: 50K–100K+ R$/day.
  - 10K CCU: social roleplay $50K–$120K/yr; competitive RPG $500K–$1M+/yr.
  - 100K CCU: $500K–$2M/month.
  - The article contains arithmetic inconsistencies, so treat it as directional.
  — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-roblox-income-ladder-what-developers-really-earn)
- **1K–5K CCU genre estimates:** Simulators about $2,000–$8,000/mo, Tycoons about $1,500–$6,000/mo, Obbies about $500–$2,000/mo. — [RoLearn 10K CCU guide](https://rolearn.dev/insights/roblox-10k-ccu-milestone-guide) / [RoLearn creator earnings](https://rolearn.dev/insights/roblox-creator-earnings-how-much-developers-make)
- **1K CCU milestone:** 100K–500K Robux/mo ($350–$1,750). — [RoLearn](https://rolearn.dev/insights/roblox-10k-ccu-milestone-guide)
- **Grow a Garden (third-party estimates):**
  - About $1.75M/month (profitable.app), about $28K–$39K/day in July 2026, and about $285.6M lifetime from a visits-based model.
  - Peak was 21.3M CCU in May 2025.
  — [profitable.app](https://profitable.app/roblox/games/grow-a-garden); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-roblox-income-ladder-what-developers-really-earn)

### Inferences
- **Working rule of thumb:** monthly developer net ≈ CCU × $0.4–$3, where $0.4 is weak monetization and $3 is a well-tuned simulator.
- Per day, that is CCU × $0.012–$0.10, consistent with ARPDAU $0.003–$0.025 at DAU ≈ 4 × CCU.
- Simulators, tycoons and progression RPGs with repeatable consumables sit at the top. Obbies and social hangouts sit at the bottom.
- These are simple, low-cost genres (simulator, tycoon, "grow/collect/steal" idle loops). They monetize best per CCU because core-loop multipliers and consumables give players a reason to pay again and again.
- Note from Q2 2026: Roblox's discovery algorithm now favors retention. Very spiky viral games may get less algorithmic support than in 2025.
