Research date: 2026-10-11. All CCU (concurrent users) figures come from third-party trackers (RBLXSTAT, Rolimon's, GGAID, RoWatcher, RoVitals, Profitable). These trackers often disagree with each other, sometimes by a lot. Older data is labeled with its date.

## Q2. Which genre and mechanic trends are rising or fading? What were the 2025 mega-hits, and are those trends still alive in late 2026?

### Takeaway
- **Rising (Aug–Oct 2026):**
  - "Steal-a-X" plus egg/pet collection, which is the Steal a Brainrot formula reskinned.
  - "+1 [stat] per second" escape/obby games, such as the Keyboard Escape series and Stone Skipping.
  - Seasonal Halloween co-op horror.
- **Fading but not dead:** the 2025 mega-hits. Their CCU has fallen 95–99%+ from their peaks, but most still run at 100K–350K.
- **Biggest collapse:** Grow a Garden (original). Its 2025 peak was 16.5M–22M CCU; by July 2026 it was at 31K–48K. Its sequel had a big launch in June 2026 but has left the October top 30.

## Q3. Which trending genres are low-complexity to build?

### Takeaway
The breakout formats of 2025–2026 are among the simplest Roblox games to build. Each one has:
- a single plot or base per player,
- a server-side money-per-second tick,
- a shop or eggs with RNG rarity,
- server-authoritative "steal" prompts, or a linear obby with a passive speed stat.

A solo developer can build the core in days to a few weeks. The real costs are live-ops cadence, which is critical because hits that stop updating die within weeks, plus thumbnails/marketing and exploit-proof server logic. Co-op horror/survival and anime combat are considerably harder.

### Cited Findings (mechanics that determine scope)
- **Steal An Egg loop:** steal eggs → hatch pets → pets earn money → upgrade base/treadmill → steal from others. Speed is the defensive stat and can be bought or trained on a treadmill. — [Player.One](https://www.player.one/robloxs-steal-egg-beating-some-platforms-biggest-games-164086); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/roblox-cracks-down-on-doomscrolling-games-like-steal-an-egg-that-forces-players-to-watch-reels-while-running-on-a-treadmill-2000727952)
- **Grow a Garden 2 loop:** buy seeds, plant, harvest, chase mutations; night raiding; defensive plants and pets; a circular map with farms facing a shared hub. — [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-july-2026/)
- **Brainrot variety:** the genre spans lava survival, parkour, creature collectors and tower defense. This shows reskinned meme assets on top of standard templates. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)
- **Policy constraint:** reward-for-watching feed mechanics now exclude a game from the Kids and Select catalogs. — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/roblox-cracks-down-on-doomscrolling-games-like-steal-an-egg-that-forces-players-to-watch-reels-while-running-on-a-treadmill-2000727952)
- **Update cadence** determines survival: "games that stop updating die within weeks". — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)

### Inferences (complexity assessment for a solo dev or 2–3 person team; based on the described mechanics, not on a sourced dev postmortem)

| Genre | Scripting scope | Art/assets | Multiplayer complexity | Overall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "+1 Speed" escape obby / keyboard escape / stone skipping | Very low: a stat ticks up per second, rebirths, a gamepass multiplier (x2), checkpoints | Low: a linear course from primitives plus a themed skin (candy, slime, ninja) | Minimal: no player-to-player interaction | **Lowest.** A solo dev could ship in about 1–2 weeks. |
| Steal-a-X (brainrot/egg/pet) | Low–medium: plot assignment, income tick, conveyor/shop, rarity RNG, a steal ProximityPrompt with carry-back, base lock timers, offline income, DataStore | Medium: many collectible models (memes/pets), which can be low-poly or from the marketplace | Medium: the theft must be server-authoritative and exploit-proof | **Low–medium.** Solo is feasible; the content pipeline matters. |
| Grow/collect idle (garden, lemons, lucky block) | Low–medium: growth timers, mutations, shop restock timers, trading | Medium: plant and pet models plus mutation VFX | Low, or medium if raiding or trading is added | **Low–medium** |
| Ride-a-pet / pet collection sims | Medium: pet follow/ride, hatching, inventories | Medium–high: animated pets | Low | **Medium** |
| Fishing / digging sims | Medium: minigames, rods, zones, bestiary | Medium–high: many fish models and zones | Low | **Medium** |
| Co-op horror (Animal Hospital, 99 Nights, DOORS) | High: AI monsters, pathfinding, events, procedural rooms or day cycles | High: atmosphere, animation, audio | Medium–high: synchronized co-op | **High** for a solo dev; feasible for 2–3 people |
| Anime combat / tower defense | High: combat netcode, hitboxes, abilities, balance | High: VFX-heavy | High | **High** |
| Roleplay/hangout (Brookhaven-like) | Medium | High: large map, vehicles, houses | Medium | Medium–high, and network effects make it very hard to break in |

- The simplest formats (+1 obbies, Steal-a-X) are also the most cloned and have the shortest lifespans, so building them quickly favors those who can also update quickly. These are inferences.

### Gaps
- No sourced developer postmortems or team-size data for Steal An Egg, Keyboard Escape or Grow a Garden were found in this pass, so the complexity ratings are analytical judgments.

## Q4. Is each trend saturated with clones? Is there evidence of clones succeeding?

### Takeaway
Every viral format attracts thousands of clones within weeks, and most die. The "Steal a ___" format alone had ~3,300 live games by June 2026. Even so, a handful of derivatives have reached the top charts:
- Steal An Egg (Jul 2026), itself a mechanic clone of Steal a Brainrot, became #1 with a multi-million-CCU peak.
- Break and Steal an Egg (5 Sep 2026) reached #8 with a 936K record.
- Escape Tsunami For Brainrots (Dec 2025) peaked near 5M.

A clone that succeeds usually adds a new twist or theme within weeks of the original's peak, rather than copying it exactly.

### Cited Findings
- As of June 2026, about 3,300 live "Steal a ___" games existed ("a format barely a year old"), plus ~36,600 "Obby", ~14,100 "Tycoon", ~12,400 "Brainrot" and ~11,800 "Simulator" games. "Most die quickly". — [RoWatcher, How many games](https://rowatcher.com/news/how-many-games-are-on-roblox-in-2026)
- RoWatcher claims Roblox's discovery system now demotes games whose titles, thumbnails and place files closely resemble existing games. This has not been verified against official Roblox documentation. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/how-many-games-are-on-roblox-in-2026)

### Inferences
- **Saturation:**
  - Steal-a-X and +1-speed obbies are hyper-saturated: thousands of near-identical titles, and the 3,300 "Steal a" figure predates the Steal An Egg wave.
  - Grow/garden idle is saturated and fading.
  - Co-op horror and survival have fewer clones relative to demand, likely because they are harder to build.
- **Clone success profile:**
  - Success comes from a fresh theme or setting (eggs/pets instead of brainrots, tsunami, swing, "break") plus a twist on the mechanic, launched about 1–8 weeks after the original's spike, with heavy update cadence and TikTok/YouTube marketing.
  - Straight reskins with prefixes like "[X2]" or "Ninja" usually peak in the low thousands.
- **Lag between original and clone hits:**
  - Steal a Brainrot (May 2025) → Escape Tsunami For Brainrots (Dec 2025): ~7 months, a theme reuse rather than a mechanic copy.
  - Steal a Brainrot → Steal An Egg (Jul 2026): ~14 months, a mechanic reuse.
  - Steal An Egg → Break and Steal an Egg: ~6 weeks.
  - Keyboard Escape (Jan 2026) → Slime variant (May 2026): ~4 months.

### Gaps

- No data on how quickly Steal An Egg clones appeared in aggregate, beyond Break and Steal an Egg.
