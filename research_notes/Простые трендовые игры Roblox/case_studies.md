# Case studies: Roblox hits built by solo developers or very small teams with simple scope (2024–2026)

Research date: 2026-10-11. All numbers are labeled with dates. CCU = concurrent users. Most CCU figures come from third-party trackers (Rolimons, RoMonitor, RoVitals, RoWatcher) or press. Treat them as estimates; sources often disagree by about 5–10%.

---

## Case studies: team, dev time, core loop, mechanics, monetization, peaks, virality, after-peak, clones

### Takeaway
The biggest simple hits of 2025 started tiny: Grow a Garden (one 16-year-old, first version in about 3 days), Steal a Brainrot (one main developer), and 99 Nights in the Forest (a 3-friend group). Each used an idle or survival loop that a small team can build. Each then reached 10–25M CCU only after a publisher or studio (Splitting Point, DoBig Studios) or very fast weekly live-ops scaled it. After the peaks, numbers fell steeply. By September 2026, Grow a Garden had dropped from 22M CCU to about 20–40K.

### Cited Findings

**Grow a Garden (GaG). Gardening idle game, released March 26, 2025**
- Team: created by an anonymous 16-year-old known as "BMWLux". Jandel (Splitting Point) says the initial version took "only three days" to build — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Splitting Point Studios bought a share in April 2025, when the game had about 1,000 CCU. The studio is remote-first and based in New Zealand, with about 19 devs; it put "almost all our staff (around 12 people)" on the game. BMWLux still contributes creatively. Do Big Studios is a quiet co-publisher — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu)
- Ownership: the original developer owns about 50%, with DoBig holding a minority share — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden); [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/dj4lxef8r) says "about half", described as an estimate
- Core loop: buy seeds with Sheckles, plant, harvest, sell, then buy progressively more exotic seeds. Crops grow while you are offline — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Mechanics: rotating seed-shop stock; weekly exclusive items that must be claimed while online; pets from loot boxes; crop stealing; trading (including real-money trading via eBay and Discord, which breaks Roblox ToS) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Design notes: non-competitive, simple start, shared multiplayer world with chat where veteran players gift fruit to newbies. Weekly updates go live every Saturday at 7am PST. Limited-time events include named mutations such as "Bloodlit" — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu)
- Monetization: Robux to skip waiting, buy perks, and steal others' crops; loot-box pets. Critics say DoBig added excessive monetization. Jandel declines to share revenue — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Revenue: about $12M in May 2025 alone from virtual items (gross) — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/dj4lxef8r)
- CCU history (2025), mostly tied to Saturday updates:
  - about 5M on May 18 ("Blood Moon")
  - 11.7M+ on May 31 ("Bizzy Bees")
  - 16M+ on June 14
  - 21.3M on June 21 ("Summer Update"), which fell to 7.6M within an hour
  - 22.3M+ on Aug 23, during the mock "admin war" with Steal a Brainrot
  - Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Typical CCU between spikes (around mid-2025): 1.5–2.5M — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu)
- Visits: fastest Roblox game to reach 1B visits (33 days). 35.3B visits and about 10.9M favourites as of May 2026. About 35% of players are under 13, per Jandel — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Virality: driven mostly by short-form video, with little long-form content at first (per NYT, cited in Wikipedia). Other drivers were the admin war and a Travis Kelce in-game event on July 26, 2025 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Bot controversy: TD Cowen alleged bot inflation, citing account surges in the Philippines and Indonesia, then retracted the manipulation claim. Roblox disputed it — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- After the peak: lost its CCU record to Steal a Brainrot in September 2025 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
  - Late September 2026: about 19.3K CCU live, 24h average about 29.7K, and a high of 166.7K on Aug 15, 2026 since tracking began on Aug 11, 2026. The 30-day average was down 6.7% — [RoVitals](https://rovitals.com/game/126884695634066)
- Clones: an unofficial Fortnite Creative replica exists — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)

**Steal a Brainrot (SaB). Idle game plus stealing, released May 16, 2025**
- Team: developed by SpyderSammy ("Sammy"), owned or published by DoBig Studios — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
  - A search summary said development took about 4 months, but I could not verify this in a primary source
  - Bloomberg names the developers as "Spyder Games and Speedy Simulator Gaming" — [Bloomberg, Mar 24 2026](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-24/roblox-s-hit-game-steal-a-brainrot-battles-its-many-imitators) (paywalled; search snippet only)
- Core loop: buy "Brainrots" (voxel Italian-brainrot meme characters) from a conveyor belt; they generate passive income in your base. Steal other players' Brainrots in a capture-the-flag style loop. Defend with a timed shield button, traps, and items. Rebirth resets progress for multipliers — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Monetization: gear sold for Robux or soft currency. Polygon criticized it because "the best items are only available for cash" and server-admin powers can be bought. Admin-abuse events drop exclusive characters — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- CCU history:
  - 5M by July 2025
  - 20M on Aug 23, 2025 (admin war; the Roblox platform record that day was 47.4M)
  - 24M on Sep 13, 2025 (Extinct event)
  - 25.4M in October 2025, the first game ever above 25M
  - Average about 1M CCU in September 2025
  - 12.8M during the Bruno Mars concert in January 2026
  - Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players/) (25.4M on Oct 7, 2025)
- Visits: about 7B by July 2025 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot). Lootbar claims 60B, an aggregator figure I did not verify — [Lootbar](https://www.lootbar.com/blog/en/steal-a-brainrot-roblox-top-charts.html)
- Virality: TikTok and YouTube stealing and strategy clips with tens of millions of views; viral reaction videos of kids upset after being robbed — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Brand expansion: Epic licensed an official Fortnite island, "Steal the Brainrot" (July 2025), which peaked at about 500K CCU on Sep 13, 2025. A film adaptation (Story Kitchen with DoBig) was announced in January 2026 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- IP risk: the Tung Tung Tung Sahur character was removed in September 2025 after a licensing dispute and returned on Nov 29, 2025 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Cheating: widespread automation scripts for collecting cash and stealing (GameRant, cited in Wikipedia) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Clones: since November 2025, the developers have filed at least 4 lawsuits against imitators — [Bloomberg, Mar 2026](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-24/roblox-s-hit-game-steal-a-brainrot-battles-its-many-imitators) (per search summary). In October 2025, Spyder Games sued the creator of the unlicensed Fortnite copy "Stealing Brainrots" — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)

**99 Nights in the Forest. Co-op survival horror**
- Team: Grandma's Favourite Games, which describes itself as "a group of 3 friends from New Zealand" (ForyxeV, Viridial, Cracky4), founded April 2020 — [Rolimons group](https://www.rolimons.com/group/6042520); [Roblox Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Grandma's_Favourite_Games)
  - Wikipedia names the individuals as Alec Kieft, Cameron Angland, and Matthew Hufton — [Wikipedia list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games)
- CCU: 11.22M on Sep 13, 2025 — [RblxTracker on X](https://x.com/RblxTracker/status/1966929846019494053). All-time high 14,153,173 on Oct 4, 2025 — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/79546208627805); 14.2M per [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/with-a-peak-player-count-of-14-2-million-99-nights-in-the-forest-has-an-audience-other-multiplayer-games-would-kill-for-to-find-these-behemoth-playerbases-you-need-to-be-on-a-platform-like-roblox/)
- Gap: dev time, monetization details, and post-peak CCU were not retrieved. The general description (survive 99 nights in a forest while a monster hunts the group) is from the game's fan wiki and was not fully read — [Fandom](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/99_Nights_in_the_Forest:_Game)

**Dead Rails. Wild West zombie survival "on rails" (2025)**
- Developer: RCM Games — [Roblox Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/RCM_Games/Dead_Rails). Team size not found.
- Core loop: drive and defend a steam train through an 1899 zombie apocalypse; scavenge fuel and parts; fight with train-mounted turrets — [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/dead-rails-and-the-hit-makers-formula)
- Growth: Roblox's own TikTok, Instagram, and X promoted it in late February 2025 (TikTok clip: 499K+ likes). Flamingo (11M subs) posted a video with 2M+ views, and KreekCraft, Thinknoodles, and Sketch also covered it. It entered the Top Trending and Up-and-Coming sorts — [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/dead-rails-and-the-hit-makers-formula)
- CCU: about 1M in March 2025, maxing out available CPU capacity — [Roblox Newsroom, Jun 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/06/roblox-infrastructure-supporting-record-breaking-games). All-time high 1,476,198 on Apr 19, 2025 — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/116495829188952)
- After the peak: around 10–20K players daily as of March 2026 — [Roblox Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/RCM_Games/Dead_Rails)

**Fisch. Skill-based fishing game, released Oct 5, 2024**
- Team: created by WoozyNate, a solo Canadian developer per the fan wiki. Moved to the "Fisching" group on Nov 4, 2024 after DoBig Studios acquired it — [Fischipedia](https://fischipedia.org/wiki/Fisch) (per search summary)
- CCU: #1 game on Roblox by CCU on Nov 30, 2024. All-time high 1,273,618 on Oct 18, 2025 — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/16732694052)
- Visits: 3B, peaked above 500K CCU (mid-2025 snapshot), "scaled by Do Big Studios" — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu)
- After the peak: after the sale to DoBig, "loaded with aggressive microtransactions", the player base collapsed. It recovered once the original creator regained ownership and removed the aggressive monetization, and is now back in the top 20 (late 2025) — [MaxPowerGaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/fish-it-how-a-simplified-copycat-became-one-of-roblox-s-biggest-hits)

**Fish It. Simplified Fisch clone (2025)**
- How it differs from Fisch (MaxPowerGaming):
  - auto-clicker-style fishing with almost no fail state, instead of Fisch's balance minigame
  - guided navigation with quest arrows and tightly spaced islands
  - auto-equip and infinite bobbers
  - rarity shown as "1 in X" odds instead of weight
  - AFK auto-fish mode, with average sessions of about 30 minutes
  - rarest fish (the Megaladon) at 1 in 4M odds
- Average CCU rose about 20x in 4 months: about 50K in August 2025, 186K in September, 389K in October, 839K in November, and more than 1M in early December 2025. Peak above 2.7M, #6 all-time on Rolimons — [MaxPowerGaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/fish-it-how-a-simplified-copycat-became-one-of-roblox-s-biggest-hits)
- Bot rumors (flat curve, about two-thirds of Discord messages from Indonesian accounts) were never confirmed. November 2025 data shows clear weekend spikes — [MaxPowerGaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/fish-it-how-a-simplified-copycat-became-one-of-roblox-s-biggest-hits)
- Team and dev time were not stated in the source.

**Plants vs Brainrots, renamed "Plants & Brainrots" in April 2026. Tower defense plus brainrot plus garden (2025)**
- Concept: Plants vs Zombies-style tower defense combined with key mechanics from Steal a Brainrot and Grow a Garden. Grow plants, kill brainrots, earn money, buy more plants — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/who-made-plants-vs-brainrots); [Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Yo_Gurt_Studio/Plants_Vs_Brainrots)
- Developer: sources conflict:
  - Sportskeeda: Yo Gurt Studios (Armin and Denis), with Jandel posting announcements in its Discord
  - Rolimons and Fandom: "Splitting Point - PVB" — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/127742093697776)
- All-time high CCU: 8,654,525. Date not confirmed; the Rolimons layout was ambiguous — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/127742093697776)

**Escape Tsunami For Brainrots. Runner/escape plus brainrot collection (December 2025 to 2026), a notable 2026 breakout**
- Developer group "Wave of Brainrots", by creator sc7ry, whose earlier game was "Steal a Character". Inspired by Steal a Brainrot and "Be Crushed by a Speeding Wall". Part of the game was sold to DoBig Studios, which drew criticism — [Roblox Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Wave_of_Brainrots/Escape_Tsunami_For_Brainrots)
- Launched December 2025. 5.7B+ visits. All-time high 4,996,032 CCU on Jan 24, 2026. A later snapshot showed 38,271 — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/131623223084840)
- Criticized for lack of originality, unanimated models, and lower quality than the creator's earlier game — [Roblox Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Wave_of_Brainrots/Escape_Tsunami_For_Brainrots)
- Peak was about 1.25M per [Earnaldo 2026](https://earnaldo.com/blog/plants-vs-brainrots-vs-escape-tsunami-for-brainrots), which conflicts with Rolimons' about 5M

**Platform context**
- About 2,000 new experiences launch on Roblox daily. Roughly 1 in 50,000 reaches the top 10 by player spend in a given year (Offpath) — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu)
- RDC 2025 data (June 2024 to July 2025): the top 1,000 developers averaged about $980K per year; the median developer earned $1,440 per year. Creator Rewards replaced Engagement-Based Payouts on July 24, 2025 (per search summary of gamedevreports and others) — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/roblox-top-100-roblox-developers)

### Inferences
- The tiny-team origin story is real but incomplete. Grow a Garden had about 1,000 CCU when a 12-person studio took over, and the 1,000x growth happened under studio live-ops. A solo dev should plan to reach "proof of traction" (about 1K CCU with good retention), then either scale fast or partner.
- The Saturday weekly update plus timed events plus limited stock pattern produced every Grow a Garden record. The record dates line up with named updates.
- Peaks are spiky. Grow a Garden fell from 21.3M to 7.6M within an hour of an update. Long-run levels are 1–10% of peak CCU within about a year.
- Lower complexity widens the audience. Fish It beat Fisch by stripping out the skill element. That is the clearest documented "simplify an existing hit" playbook.

### Gaps
- Dev time for Steal a Brainrot (about 4 months is unverified), 99 Nights, Dead Rails, Fish It, and Plants vs Brainrots.
- Exact team sizes for RCM Games (Dead Rails) and the Fish It developer.
- Reliable 2026 CCU for Steal a Brainrot and 99 Nights.
- Hard data on simple tycoons, incremental clickers, social hangout/RP games, and "Find the ___" games from 2024–2026. I found no reliable stats for these within the call budget.
- Dig: unclear whether "DIG 💛" is the game the brief means.

## Common traits of the simple hits

### Takeaway
The recurring formula has five parts: passive or idle income that keeps growing offline, rarity and gacha collectibles, social interaction (gifting, stealing, co-op survival), a meme or brainrot IP that spreads easily as short-form video, and very frequent timed live events (weekly drops, admin-abuse events). Low production values are tolerated; Escape Tsunami hit 5M CCU despite unanimated models.

### Cited Findings
- Offline growth ("Grows Offline"), non-competitive design, simple start, gifting in a shared world, and weekly Saturday updates — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu)
- Rotating seed stock and weekly exclusives that must be claimed while online, plus pet loot boxes — [Wikipedia GaG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Passive-income meme characters, stealing, shields and traps, rebirth, and admin-abuse events — [Wikipedia SaB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Viral clips built on social conflict (being robbed, kids crying) — [Wikipedia SaB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Short-form video as the main channel — [Wikipedia GaG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- "1 in X" rarity odds, which kids understand easily; AFK modes; frictionless UX — [MaxPowerGaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/fish-it-how-a-simplified-copycat-became-one-of-roblox-s-biggest-hits)
- "Most Advanced Yet Acceptable": familiar format with a novel setting; platform sorts (Up-and-Coming, Top Trending) plus YouTuber social proof — [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/dead-rails-and-the-hit-makers-formula)
- Low production values tolerated: unanimated models and "lower quality" at 5M CCU — [Roblox Fandom: Escape Tsunami](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Wave_of_Brainrots/Escape_Tsunami_For_Brainrots); [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/131623223084840)
- Young audience: about 35% of Grow a Garden players are under 13 — [Wikipedia GaG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)

### Inferences
- For a solo dev, the most reusable building blocks are:
  - a timer-based passive-income object (plant or brainrot)
  - rarity tiers with odds shown as "1 in X"
  - a shop with rotating stock
  - one social-conflict verb (steal) or social-help verb (gift)
  - a weekly event cadence
- Toolbox-quality or low-poly assets appear acceptable for this genre.

### Gaps
- No direct sourced evidence on Toolbox/Creator Store asset usage in these specific games.

## Hybrid / genre-twist combos that worked

### Takeaway
Most 2025–2026 mega-hits are explicit mashups of two earlier hits or mechanics. Steal a Brainrot (idle tycoon plus capture-the-flag stealing plus meme IP), Plants vs Brainrots (Plants vs Zombies tower defense plus Grow a Garden plus Steal a Brainrot, about 8.65M CCU peak), and Escape Tsunami For Brainrots (Speeding Wall runner plus Steal a Brainrot, about 5M CCU peak in January 2026) are documented examples.

### Cited Findings
- Steal a Brainrot: passive-income idle game plus stealing "compared to capture the flag" plus Italian-brainrot meme — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- Plants vs Brainrots: "strongly inspired by Plants vs Zombies", combining tower defense with key mechanics from Steal a Brainrot and Grow a Garden. Peak 8,654,525 CCU — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/who-made-plants-vs-brainrots); [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/127742093697776)
- Escape Tsunami For Brainrots: inspired by Steal a Brainrot and "Be Crushed by a Speeding Wall". Peak 4,996,032 CCU on Jan 24, 2026 — [Roblox Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Wave_of_Brainrots/Escape_Tsunami_For_Brainrots); [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/131623223084840)
- Dead Rails: familiar survival format plus a novel Wild West train setting — [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/dead-rails-and-the-hit-makers-formula)
- Grow a Garden: gardening idle plus pet collection plus crop stealing — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- "+1 Speed Every Second": speed simulator plus obby plus egg hatching and pets — [Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/17172684646/1-Speed-Every-Second)

### Inferences
- Combining the current top hit's IP or theme with an older proven genre structure is the dominant pattern. Examples: brainrot plus tower defense, brainrot plus wall runner, garden plus stealing.
- Timing matters. The mashups appeared while the parent games were at peak attention, from mid-2025 to early 2026.

### Gaps
- No controlled data on hybrid versus non-hybrid success rates; the evidence is survivorship-biased.

## Acquisitions by larger studios and implications

### Takeaway
Studio buy-ins at low CCU are the norm for the biggest simple hits. Splitting Point bought into Grow a Garden at about 1K CCU. DoBig Studios co-owns or publishes Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, Fisch, and part of Escape Tsunami. Owners often keep large stakes (about 50% in Grow a Garden's case). The risk is monetization creep, which has demonstrably killed retention (Fisch).

### Cited Findings
- Splitting Point bought a share of Grow a Garden in April 2025 at about 1K CCU and assigned about 12 staff. Its portfolio was about 10B visits, with 3 titles over 1B — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-grow-a-gardens-89-million-ccu)
- BMWLux keeps about 50% — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- Since 2021, Madsen's team has made 25+ Roblox games (Business Insider via search summary) — [Calcalist / search summary](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/dj4lxef8r)
- DoBig Studios (Mobile Game Advertising & More, Inc., CEO John Cannata, founded 2020) mainly acquires Roblox games. It has been criticized for aggressive monetization, limited-time spending pressure, randomized rewards, and opaque acquisitions — [Roblox Fandom: Do Big Studios](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Do_Big_Studios%E2%84%A2); [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/do-big-studios)
- Fisch, after DoBig: aggressive microtransactions led to player collapse. It recovered after the creator regained ownership and removed them — [MaxPowerGaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/fish-it-how-a-simplified-copycat-became-one-of-roblox-s-biggest-hits)
- Escape Tsunami's partial sale to DoBig drew criticism — [Roblox Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Wave_of_Brainrots/Escape_Tsunami_For_Brainrots)

### Inferences
- A small dev's realistic path is to build the prototype fast and get to about 1K CCU with a viral loop. Partnering then gives them live-ops staff, ad spend, and admin-event cross-promotion (for example the GaG versus SaB "admin war").
- Keep equity and creative control. Fisch shows that a monetization-heavy partner can destroy a game.

### Gaps
- Deal prices and revenue splits are not public for any of these deals.

## Failure cases: clones that flopped and why

### Takeaway
The market is saturated with brainrot and template clones. Lawsuits, template-store copies, and an anti-"brainrot slop" backlash are documented. But I found no sourced post-mortems of specific flopped clones, and there is a notable counter-example: Fish It, a clone that beat its original. Documented "failure" patterns are post-peak collapse (Dead Rails about 1.5M to 10–20K; Grow a Garden 22M to about 30K) and monetization-driven decline (Fisch).

### Cited Findings
- The popularity created "a flood of brain-rotting slop" — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/brainrot-meme-fortnite-roblox-playstation-plus-odst-helldivers-2-news-2000618411)

### Inferences
- A pure re-skin of a hit enters a crowded field with legal risk, since Spyder Games litigates. Clones that succeed change the experience meaningfully: simpler (Fish It) or a genre mashup (Plants vs Brainrots).
- Meme-IP lifespans are short, about 2–6 months at peak. Plan to cash in fast or build loops that survive the meme's fading.
