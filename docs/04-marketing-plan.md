# Meteor Rush — Promotion & Marketing Plan

Version 0.1 · 2026-10-11 · Budget assumption: small (ads ≤ ~$300–600 in first month), time-rich solo team.

## 1. Positioning
- **Hook:** "Every second you get faster. Run to the edge of the crater, grab the rarest meteor, and don't get robbed on the way home."
- **Emotion:** speed rush + "1 in 2,000!" reveal + chase drama.
- **Not:** a "Steal a ___" clone, brainrot, or reskin. Never copy leaders' titles, thumbnails or models (Home demotes look-alike metadata; legal risk).

## 2. Store page (the #1 lever)
- **Thumbnail Personalization A/B** (Roblox avg +8.5% PTR, up to +50%): always 3–4 live variants; replace the loser every 2 weeks.
  Variants: (a) chase-back close-up, (b) Mythic hatch with "1 in 2,000", (c) shower race crowd, (d) crew station at night.
  Rule: no clickbait that the first minute can't deliver — bounce < 60 s is a top signal.
- **Gameplay video** 30 s: 0–3 s speed counter exploding, 3–15 s rare grab + chase, 15–30 s hatch reveal + station income.
- **Icon:** single creature face + speed lines, readable at 50 px. Test seasonal icon variants.
- **Title:** "Meteor Rush" (+ seasonal tag in brackets only during events, e.g. "[❄️ WINTER] Meteor Rush").

## 3. Roblox-native channels
| Channel | Action | When |
|---|---|---|
| Ads Manager (Plays objective) | Small always-on budget; retarget lapsed players once audience ≥ 20k | From soft launch |
| Today's Picks | Nominate as new game | Pre-launch, re-nominate on major updates |

| Experience notifications | 13+ opted-in, personal + actionable | Launch |
| Private servers (free) | "Play with crew" invites → friends signal | Launch |
| Events/badges | Seasonal badges for collectors | Each event |

## 4. Short-form video (primary organic growth; Grow a Garden grew via TikTok/Shorts)
Content pillars (post 1/day on TikTok + YouTube Shorts, 3/week minimum):
1. **Chase clips** — thief vs owner, last-second catch (recorded on dev alt accounts / with consent from friends).
2. **Rarity reveals** — "I found a 1 in 2,000 Mythic".
3. **Speed escalation** — 0 → 10,000 speed timelapse.
4. **Shower chaos** — 8 players racing.
5. **Update teasers** — Thursday/Friday before Saturday update.
Tools: in-game replay-friendly camera mode (free cam toggle for admins), consistent caption template, game name in first frame.

## 5. Creators
- Target small Roblox YouTubers/TikTokers (5k–200k subs) in simulator/obby niche; offer: early access, personal creator code (codes system), custom creature naming.
- Wave 1: 10–20 creators at soft launch week 2 (after FTUE fixes). Wave 2: after Dec 21 scale decision.
- Never pay for misleading content; disclose sponsorships per platform rules.
- Brand-integration fees (2027 Roblox revenue share) — out of scope.

## 6. Community
- Discord (13+ only, per Discord ToS): #announcements, #codes, #bugs, #suggestions, #trading-off (no trading in MVP).
- Weekly cadence: Mon patch notes recap, Wed midweek event ping, Fri teaser, Sat update + Starfall ping.
- Codes: released in update notes and on socials; expire in 7–14 days to drive return visits.

## 8. Measurement
- Per variant: PTR, bounce bands, session length (Creator Analytics).
- Per creator/code: code redemptions as attribution.
- Ads: cost per play; stop campaigns whose players bounce > organic baseline.
- Monthly: share of traffic from Home vs search vs ads vs friends.

## 9. Compliance
- Accurate metadata; no implied affiliation with other games/creators; no "free Robux" claims.
- No ads/rewarded video to under-13; no "watch to earn" mechanics.
- Respect COPPA-style norms: never collect personal data in Discord/forms from minors.
